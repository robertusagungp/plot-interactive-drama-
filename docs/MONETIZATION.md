# PLOT Monetization & Entitlement Architecture

## 1. Monetization Philosophy
- **Additive, Not Punitive**: The free path is emotionally rich, coherent, and satisfying.
- **Premium Value**: Premium currency (💎 Diamonds) unlocks exclusive intimate moments, bonus POV, alternate timelines, and fast episode bingeing.

## 2. Currency & Economy Model
- **Coins (🪙)**: Used for unlocking episodes beyond Episode 3 (10–25 Coins/ep).
- **Diamonds (💎)**: Used for premium romantic choices, bonus POV scenes, and special dialogue branches (10 Diamonds/choice).
- **IDR Packages**: Real Indonesian pricing via QRIS, GoPay, OVO, and Dana (Rp 10.000, Rp 25.000, Rp 50.000, Rp 100.000).

## 3. PLOT+ Subscription Tier
- Unlimited episode access without coin deductions
- 3 to 5 simultaneous Story Timeline Save Slots
- Bonus POV and early access episodes
- Ad-free reading experience
