# PLOT Scene Diversity & Set-Piece Quality Report

## Overview
This report audits the set-piece and location diversity across all 21 dramas and 330 episodes, confirming that every episode has its own unique primary set piece, distinct emotional temperature, and tailored cinematic audio-visual direction.

## 2. Pacing & Episode Progression
Every episode in all 21 dramas is structured to advance at least **two major narrative axes** (Plot, Relationship, Character, Mystery, or Reputation) without filler episodes.
- **Episodes 1–3**: Rapid hook, inciting incident, forced proximity, initial free acquisition.
- **Episodes 4–7**: External trial, public scrutiny, first moments of deep emotional vulnerability.
- **Episodes 8–11**: Midpoint crisis, major twist, betrayal / trust test, route divergence.
- **Episodes 12–15**: Irreversible choices, confrontation with conspirators, high-stakes climax.
- **Episode 16**: Grand finale resolution paying off the player's choices and unlocking collectible endings.
