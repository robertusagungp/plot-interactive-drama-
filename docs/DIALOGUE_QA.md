## 2. Indonesian Localization Standard
- **Natural Colloquial & Contemporary Drama Tone**: Using expressive everyday vocabulary (*aku/kamu*, *nggak*, *udah*, *beneran*, *banget*, *liat*, *bakal*, *hadepin*, *bikin*) tailored to character relationship dynamics.
- **Zero AI Stiff Translations**: Banned overly textbook constructs like *"Apakah kamu akan melakukan tindakan ini kepadaku?"*.
- **Subtextual Tension**: Characters communicate desires, doubts, and emotional stakes through hesitation, actions, and sharp replies rather than stating raw emotions outright.
