## 1. Aku Menikahi Musuhku (`i-married-my-enemy`)
- **Premise**: Sarah Wijaya and Adrian Hartono are heirs to rival luxury conglomerates. When a $100M joint contract forces them into a 1-year public marriage, they must survive media blitzes, boardroom betrayals, and the sudden realization that their hatred was masking deep, unspoken passion.
- **Core Dramatic Question**: Can two sworn enemies build genuine trust when their marriage began as a billion-dollar lie?
- **Protagonist**: Sarah Wijaya (25, Fierce, Brilliant Strategist, Protects family legacy).
- **Lead Hero**: Adrian Hartono (28, Cold Billionaire CEO, Dry wit, Secretly devoted protector).
- **Antagonist**: Vanessa Lim (Adrian's manipulative ex-fiancée seeking to void the merger).
- **Motif & Setting**: Penthouse Balcony at 2 AM, Rain over Jakarta Skyline, Glass Boardrooms.

---

## 2. I Woke Up Married to Korea's Coldest Idol (`i-woke-up-married-to-koreas-coldest-idol`)
- **Premise**: Stylist Han Ji-woo wakes up in Las Vegas with a gold ring on her finger next to Kang Tae-jun, the notoriously cold leader of Asia's biggest idol group. To protect both their careers from explosive media scandals, they maintain a secret contract marriage while navigating world tours and dorm rules.
- **Core Dramatic Question**: How can an ordinary stylist protect Korea's biggest superstar when their secret marriage is one flash away from destruction?
- **Protagonist**: Han Ji-woo (24, Sharp Stylist, Creative, Loyal).
- **Lead Hero**: Kang Tae-jun (26, Top K-Pop Idol, Perfectionist, Protective of his private life).
- **Antagonist**: Seo Min-ah (Rival idol agency director seeking to leak the Vegas marriage certificate).
- **Motif & Setting**: Backstage Dressing Room, Stage Flares, Sunglasses & Face Masks in Neon Alleys.

---

## 3. My Fake Boyfriend Is Actually a Chaebol Heir (`my-fake-boyfriend-is-actually-a-chaebol-heir`)
- **Premise**: Desperate to attend her cheating ex's wedding with a date, Han Ji-woo hires a $200 rental boyfriend named Lee Min-hyuk. She is shocked when he arrives in a convoy of Maybachs, revealing he is the hidden crown prince of LK Group.
- **Core Dramatic Question**: What happens when a fake $200 rental date turns into a battle for a conglomerate dynasty?
- **Protagonist**: Han Ji-woo (25, Hardworking Designer, Witty, Resourceful).
- **Lead Hero**: Lee Min-hyuk (27, Chaebol Heir, Playful charm hiding immense family expectations).
- **Antagonist**: Choi Ara (Min-hyuk's ambitious stepmother plotting to disinherit him).
- **Motif & Setting**: Maybach Fleet, Grand Wedding Halls, Five-Star Luxury Rooftops.

---

## 4. The CEO Who Fired Me Became My Fiancé (`the-ceo-who-fired-me-became-my-fiance`)
- **Premise**: Fired on Monday morning by her tyrannical CEO Yoon Jin-hyuk, Da-eun is dragged by her parents to an arranged marriage dinner on Friday—only to find Jin-hyuk sitting across the table, equally shocked.
- **Core Dramatic Question**: Can a fired secretary turn the tables when her ruthless former boss becomes her legally betrothed partner?
- **Protagonist**: Yoon Da-eun (26, Tenacious Secretary, Never backs down).
- **Lead Hero**: Yoon Jin-hyuk (29, Authoritarian CEO, Awkward and sweet when alone).
- **Antagonist**: Han Yu-jin (Corporate director scheming to expose their private marriage contract).
- **Motif & Setting**: Executive Corner Office, High-End French Restaurant, Family Boardroom.

---

## 5. I Accidentally Texted a Superstar at 2 A.M. (`i-accidentally-texted-a-superstar-at-2-am`)
- **Premise**: A late-night typo sends Cha Min-seo's unfiltered design rants directly to global music icon Do Sun-woo. Unbeknownst to her, her honesty becomes Sun-woo's only sanctuary in a world of fake industry flattery.
- **Core Dramatic Question**: Can a late-night wrong number become the safest haven for the world's loneliest superstar?
- **Protagonist**: Cha Min-seo (23, Design Student, Honest, Creative).
- **Lead Hero**: Do Sun-woo (25, Global Solo Artist, Exhausted by fame, Yearns for sincere connection).
- **Antagonist**: Agency Director Kang (Controlling talent manager tracking every phone record).
- **Motif & Setting**: Blue Screen Midnight Texts, Soundproof Recording Studio, Rooftop Night Air.
